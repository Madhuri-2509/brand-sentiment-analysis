# brand-sentiment-analysis

Customer reviews have emerged as a strong source of information in the digital era, which affects brand
perception and consumer decision. The presence of millions of reviews created by users on the e-commerce
platforms like Amazon offers insightful information on customer preferences, products quality, and overall customer
satisfaction. To prevent businesses falling behind in terms of competition and customer focus, this mass of textual
data, primarily unstructured, requires a look into. The project is expected to create a machine learning-based
project relying on Natural Language Processing (NLP) methods to analyze Amazon reviews and derive meaningful
information about customers. The system will be used to analyze sentiments, classify the text and extract the topics
in order to know about how the customers feel and trends in their responses. We can also consider advanced NLP
models like word embeddings and transformer-based models which can be used to increase the accuracy of the
data analysis. The projected result of this project is to have a complete analytical model that assists business to be
aware about customer sentiment, product problems and improve decision-making. Using insights that are based on
the data, the organizations in question can enhance their customer satisfaction as well as brand perception, along
with making their marketing better.
